# Neo4j AI Usage Policy for Interview Processes (reference)

Owner: Talent Acquisition. Applies to all candidates, interviewers, and
hiring team members.

## Acceptable AI Use — For Candidates

**May:**
- Use AI tools to prepare for interviews — practicing technical questions,
  revising resumes, studying Neo4j products/concepts.
- Use AI to generate code samples or test queries, **provided the candidate
  can explain the logic, understanding, and reasoning during the
  interview.**

**Must not:**
- Use AI to generate real-time answers during a live interview or
  assessment unless explicitly allowed.
- Misrepresent AI-generated work as personal understanding or experience.

## AI Usage in Engineering Technical Stages

Neo4j expects candidates to explain, understand, and stand behind submitted
work, regardless of whether external tools were used for research. Focus of
assessment: thinking, problem-solving approach, code quality, structure,
and ability to adapt the solution when asked to extend or modify it.

AI-powered tools are recognised as part of modern engineering workflows and
their use is encouraged for exploration — but the work must reflect the
candidate's own understanding and approach.

**AI usage is not permitted during live coding.**

## Transparency and Disclosure

If a candidate relies on AI assistance in creating submitted materials, they
should note the use of such tools.

## Monitoring and Compliance

Neo4j reserves the right to audit AI usage in hiring processes. Violation
for candidates: disqualification from the recruitment process.
